01 / About MELTS

Big questions.
Connected answers.

MELTS brings five disciplines into one curious community. Students explore how medicine, engineering, law, technology, and science work together to understand the world and shape its future.

Our mission

Make interdisciplinary learning accessible, hands-on, and genuinely exciting.

Our vision

A generation of young thinkers ready to connect ideas and solve meaningful problems.
